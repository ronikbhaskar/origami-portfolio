I feel like this is the kind of thing you would see in a dream. You're cutting down a tree, and as you step back, it was actually a person. Or maybe you were the tree that was cut down. Interpretation is up to you, I took a lot of inspiration from surrealism, particularly the metamorphic work. I've also received a lot of comments from people wishing that they could wear these pants, so maybe something to take note of if you're a fashion designer.
